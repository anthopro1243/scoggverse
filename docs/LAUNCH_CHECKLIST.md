# Final playtest checklist (Milestone 5)

The automated playtest covers the rules, every control, the art and every sound's timing. These are the things only a person can check. About 15 minutes; no FNAF skill needed. Use the preview link from the PR, or the live site after "merge".

## On a laptop (Chrome, Edge or Firefox), sound on

- [ ] The page shows "FIVE NIGHTS AT SCOGGY'S" with a red loading bar and "Loading N of 53 MB", then the title. Under 30 s on home Wi-Fi.
- [ ] The title shows the "I WANT YOU... to not deny yourself an education" poster, and the menu music plays after the first click.
- [ ] Settings → Sound check…: press a few buttons, including Door slam (left speaker), Power-out march, Scream: Uncle Scogg and Menu music. Anything too loud, too quiet or wrong?
- [ ] Settings → Reduce flashing on, then start a night and raise the cameras: no flicker. Turn it back off.
- [ ] New Game: the "12 AM / Night 1" card, then the phone rings and Dr. Scoggins' captions appear. Mute call stops it.
- [ ] Press A and D (doors), Q and E (lights), Space (cameras), 1–9 (camera rooms). Each one reacts and makes a sound.
- [ ] CAM 6 / the yurt (CAM 5): Scoggis Khan's standee shows his new, bigger face.
- [ ] The office poster behind the desk is the new 1917 "I WANT YOU" Uncle Scogg; the Kennedy poster on the back wall has the bigger face.
- [ ] Uncle Scogg on the cameras and in the dark doorway at power out still shows his legs (the striped-pants costume).
- [ ] Credits (title screen) read right to you, and the disclaimer is at the bottom of the title.

## On your phone

- [ ] Opening the link shows "Best on a laptop" before anything downloads.
- [ ] "Play anyway" loads the game. Sideways, the title and buttons fit the screen.
- [ ] Opening it again skips the notice (it remembers).

## On the Scoggverse site (after "merge")

- [ ] Paste the snippet from `docs/EMBED.md` onto the page.
- [ ] The game shows in a 16:9 box. Clicking it, then pressing A, shuts a door (keys work inside the frame).
- [ ] Press F in the game: it goes full screen; F again comes back. The "Open it on its own page" link opens the game alone.
