How to survive

  1. Check the hall light before you close a door. Doors use up power, so only close one when someone's actually there.
  2. Keep checking CAM 5. If the yurt is open, Scoggis Khan is about to run at you.
  3. Uncle Scogg only moves when you're not looking at him.
  4. If your power runs out, it's pretty much over.
  5. Each hour is about 90 seconds. Make it to 6 AM to win and get Scogg Bucks.

The Scoggatronics

  • Scoggcrates

    Comes down the left hall. Shut the left door when you see him.

  • Scogga Lisa

    Comes down the right hall. Shut the right door.

  • Scoggis Khan

    Hides in the yurt. If you stop checking on him, he runs at the left door.

  • Uncle Scogg

    Moves last. If your power runs out, he's the one who gets you.
