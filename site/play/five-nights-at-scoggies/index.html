How to survive

Check the hall light before you shut a door, since doors eat your power. Keep an eye on CAM 5, because when the yurt is open Scoggis Khan is about to run at you. Uncle Scogg only moves when you're not looking at him. Each hour is about 90 seconds, and if you make it to 6 AM you get Scogg Bucks. If the power runs out it's pretty much over.

The Scoggatronics

  • Scoggcrates

    Comes down the left hall. Shut the left door when you see him.

  • Scogga Lisa

    Comes down the right hall. Shut the right door.

  • Scoggis Khan

    Hides in the yurt. If you stop checking on him, he runs at the left door.

  • Uncle Scogg

    Moves last. If your power runs out, he's the one who gets you.

There's also a secret one.
