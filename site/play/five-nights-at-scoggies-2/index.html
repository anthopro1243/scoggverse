How to survive

Check the light before you shut a door, since shut doors cost you. Scoggcrates and Scogga Lisa come up different stairs, so watch both. If Scoggis Khan is gone from the computer lab, shut the back door. Uncle Scogg only moves when you're not looking at him.

The Scoggatronics

  • Scoggcrates

    Comes up Stairs 1 to the hall door.

  • Scogga Lisa

    Comes up Stairs 2 to the back door.

  • Scoggis Khan

    Hides in the computer lab. If he's gone, shut the back door.

  • Uncle Scogg

    Moves when you're not watching.
