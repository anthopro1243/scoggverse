Scogg Boy & Scogg Girl

Two player. He grabs the Scogg Bucks, she grabs the Gavels.

No jump scares. Don't worry.

Open the game on its own page

How to play

Scogg Boy uses A, D and W. Scogg Girl uses the arrow keys. He grabs the Scogg Bucks and she grabs the Gavels, and the Bucks lose value the longer you take. He can't touch Ink, she can't touch Inflation, and Recession gets you both. Plates, levers and crates open the way, then you both stand at your own door to get out. Esc pauses, R restarts the level, M turns the sound off, and two gamepads work too.
