Scogg Boy & Scogg Girl

Two player. One of you is fire, one is water.

No jump scares. Dont worry.

Open the game on its own page

You need a keyboard for this one. It won't work on a phone.

Controls

Scogg Boy
A D to move, W to jump
Scogg Girl
← → to move, ↑ to jump
Esc
Pause
R
Restart the chamber
M
Sound on or off
F
Full screen
Gamepads
Two of them work too

How to play

  1. Scogg Boy grabs the Scogg Bucks and Scogg Girl grabs the Gavels. Bucks lose value the longer you take.
  2. He can't touch Ink, she can't touch Inflation, and Recession gets you both.
  3. Plates, levers and crates open the way, and then you both stand at your own door to get out.
